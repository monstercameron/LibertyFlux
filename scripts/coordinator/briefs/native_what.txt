Your batch: the {count} script native handlers in `{list_path}`. Each entry has the native's public name, its hash, its handler's address, and `analysis`: the lane whose `natives_analysis.json` already describes what that handler does at the engine level. Native handlers are the best-understood functions in the program: each has a public name and documented behaviour (github.com/sannybuilder/library `gta_iv/gta_iv.json`, github.com/HappinessMP/natives, the gtamods wiki), each takes one pointer to a call context (argument array, argument count, return slot), and most are short: read the arguments, turn a script handle into an object through a pool lookup, call one or two engine functions or read a field, and store the result.
